About Nexo AI

Nexo AI is a multilingual AI assistant for asking questions, learning, writing, coding, translating and working with text. The interface is available in English, Arabic, French and Spanish.

It is an early-stage project. Features that are not connected to a real service, such as accounts, file uploads, image generation or voice, are not part of the service and are not represented as available.
