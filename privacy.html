Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how Nexo AI (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use this website. Questions about it can be sent to nexo859@gmail.com.

What you submit

When you send a message, the text of that message and the recent messages of your current conversation, together with the assistant mode and interface language you selected, are sent through our server to a third-party AI provider, which generates the reply. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, identity documents or other sensitive personal information.

What we do not do

  • There are no user accounts.
  • We do not store your conversations on our server. Your conversation lives in your browser tab and disappears when you start a new conversation or close or reload the page.
  • We do not sell your information.

Technical data

To deliver the service and protect it from abuse, our server and hosting provider process technical data such as your IP address and request times. We use IP addresses in the server’s memory to limit how many requests can be made per minute. Our hosting provider may keep standard access or security logs under its own policies. Our application logs error status codes but not the content of your messages.

AI provider

Your messages are processed by the AI provider we connect to. That provider handles data under its own terms and privacy policy, which may allow it to retain submitted content for a period of time. Treat anything you type as something a third party will process.

Cookies, local storage, ads and analytics

This website does not set cookies. It stores your chosen interface language in your browser’s local storage so the page opens in that language next time; you can clear it in your browser settings at any time. The website currently loads no advertising or analytics scripts. If we add them, we will update this page first and ask for consent where the law requires it.

Children

Nexo AI is not directed at children under 13. Younger users should use it only together with a parent or guardian.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not keep your conversations or accounts, there is generally nothing to retrieve or delete on our side beyond technical logs. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. To make a request, or to ask what we hold about you, write to nexo859@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
